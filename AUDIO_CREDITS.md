## Recorded event and racing audio

The event queue uses the recordings above for UI clicks, terrain footsteps, combat and goblin reactions, trade, consumption, doors, pickups, spells, healing, level-up, victory, defeat, and board-game actions. `blessing` intentionally reuses the already-credited `magic.wav` recording; `chip` and swipe/click feedback reuse `ui-click.wav`. Eating and drinking use `eat.ogg` and `drink.ogg`. No synthesized fallback is generated when a recording is unavailable.

The Car Racing District uses only the recorded files above: GGBotNet’s engine-start, low engine and horn recordings; domasx2’s public-domain engine loop; Kenney’s collision, cue, checkpoint and Starter Kit Racing skid recordings; Oculus Audio Pack 01 car-door recordings; and existing licensed result/footstep recordings. Playback-rate and volume changes are playback controls, not synthesis. Continuous loops have explicit stop lifecycle handling.
