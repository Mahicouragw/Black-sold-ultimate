4a. What this game is, and who it is for

Black Sword Ultimate is a free fantasy role-playing game built so that blind players and sighted players can play the same game together. Everything is designed to work with a screen reader such as Android TalkBack as well as on screen. You explore a world, fight monsters, complete quests, collect equipment, play board games in the Game Hall, and chat with other players.

Because blind and sighted players share the same rooms, we ask everyone to be patient and descriptive in chat, and never to mock how another person plays.

4b. How everyone should behave

  • Be kind first. Assume the other player is a real person having a harder day than you can see.
  • Be patient. Screen-reader players may take longer to read and reply. Do not rush or spam them.
  • Be descriptive. Avoid "click here" or "look at that"; say what you actually mean, so it makes sense without sight.
  • No gatekeeping. Never tell someone they cannot play because of a disability, age, gender, religion, caste, nationality or language.
  • Help newcomers. Answering a beginner's question is always welcome.
  • Play fair. Win and lose gracefully; do not brag about exploits or shame someone for losing.
  • Use any language you like, but do not use another language to hide abuse.
