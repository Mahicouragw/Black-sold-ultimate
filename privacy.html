Privacy Policy

Application: Black Sword Ultimate — an accessible fantasy role-playing game.
Effective date: 17 August 2026 · Version: 7.22.2

This policy describes only what the game actually collects. We do not use advertising networks, and the application contains no analytics or tracking scripts. We do not sell or rent your data.

1. Who we are

Black Sword Ultimate is an independent, community-oriented game project. Contact us through the in-game Send Feedback feature in Chat Rooms, or through the project repository at github.com/Mahicouragw/Black-sold-ultimate.

2. Why we ask for your details

We ask for a small amount of personal information for two specific reasons, and nothing else:

  • To save your game progress on our server, not only on your device. Your heroes, levels, inventory, quests and currency are stored in our database so that you can lose your phone, reinstall the app, or sign in on a different device and still find your adventure exactly as you left it. Local-only storage would be erased whenever you clear your browser data.
  • To verify who you are, so nobody else can take your heroes. Your name and email (through Google Sign-In) or your Player ID and PIN act as the key to your account. Without a verified identity we could not tell you apart from someone trying to claim your progress, and we could not restore your account if you were locked out.

We also use your hero name to show you to other players in chat and on the online hero list, and we keep moderation records so that abusive behaviour can be acted on. We do not use any of this for advertising or profiling.

3. What we do NOT collect

  • No advertising identifiers and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, telemetry, heat-maps, or session recording.
  • No precise location or GPS data.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, or microphone recordings. Optional voice commands are processed by your own browser/device speech service and are not recorded or uploaded by us.
  • No payment or card data. The game has no purchases; all in-game currency is fictional and earned through play.

4. Third-party services we rely on

  • Supabase — database, authentication and storage.
  • Google Sign-In — only if you choose to sign in with Google. It gives us your name and email so we can verify your account.
  • AI language provider — when you talk to an AI NPC, the text of your question is sent to a language-model provider to generate the reply. Do not type personal information into NPC conversations.
  • GitHub Pages and Vercel — website hosting. Like virtually all web hosts, they may process connection information such as IP address in their own server logs for security and abuse prevention. We do not receive or store those logs.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

5. How long we keep data

  • Chat messages: about five minutes. Expiry is enforced by the database, not merely hidden in the app.
  • Hero and save data: until you delete the hero or ask us to delete your account.
  • Feedback and moderation records: kept while needed to resolve the report and to prevent repeat abuse.
  • Device preferences and offline cache: until you clear your browser storage or uninstall the app.

6. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and you can withdraw consent by deleting your account. Send the request through Send Feedback in Chat Rooms. You may also clear all locally stored data at any time from your browser settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU/UK GDPR, or similar regional legislation. We aim to honour access and deletion requests regardless of your location.

7. Children and young players

The game is intended for a general audience. If you are under the age of digital consent in your country, please use the game with the involvement of a parent or guardian. Chat is a public, moderated space — do not share personal details such as your real name, address, school, phone number or email in chat. If we learn that a child's personal data has been collected without appropriate consent, we will delete it.

8. Security

Authentication is handled by Supabase. Recovery PINs are stored hashed, never in plain text. Database access is restricted with row-level security so players cannot read one another's private records. Your Player ID and PIN are private credentials — never share them in chat. No online service can promise perfect security, so please use a unique PIN.

9. Public versus private information

Other players can see only your public hero profile: hero name, title, class, level, public stats and public achievements. Your email address, account identifiers, Player Code, PIN and internal database IDs are never shown to other players.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and its effective date. Continuing to play after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Honest disclaimer: this policy describes our actual practices in plain language. It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. We do not claim compliance with every law in every country.
